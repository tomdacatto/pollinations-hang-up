Can two AIs end a call?

A video of two AI agents in a job interview who never learn to hang up was one of the top posts on r/ChatGPT in October. Here two models talk on the phone, each with its own role and no idea what the other was told. A call ends only when one of them replies with [HANG UP]. The page counts, in code, how many turns the call lasted, how many were goodbyes ("thanks for your time", "take care"), and the longest run of goodbyes in a row. Run every pair, with and without being told how to hang up, and see which models can leave. When nobody is told, nobody can hang up, so the goodbyes show whether the models know the call is over.
